This book is just an attempt to present and demonstrate the fundamental concepts of Microprocessors & Microcontrollers using PIC18 devices which have traditionally been taught using 8086 and 8051 architectures.

The PIC18 family of devices is a product of Microchip Technology Inc. This book references and utilizes figures, information, and content from various Microchip Technology Inc.'s documents & datasheets, most notably those related to the PIC18F458, PIC18C801 and PIC18F8720, PIC18xxK40/PIC18xxK42 device families.
